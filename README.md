# SoK: Design Patterns for Central Bank Digital Currency

This repository contains the full classification table accompanying our Systematization of Knowledge on the Central Bank Digital Currency design space.

## Paper

The paper is currently available as a preprint on arXiv:

**[Systematization of Knowledge: The Design Space of Digital Payment Systems with Potential for CBDC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16320)**

The paper will appear at the 2027 IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy (S&P).

## Repository Contents

- `extended_table.pdf` – Full classification table of the CBDC designs analyzed in the paper
